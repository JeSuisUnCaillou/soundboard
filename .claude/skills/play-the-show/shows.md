run when you can: each format leans on another part of the board.

## Formats

| Format | What it asks of the board |
| --- | --- |
| **Match d'impro**: two teams, a referee in stripes, an MC drawing themes from a hat | a whistle and a buzzer at the referee's sign, a theme jingle for each draw, a time-out loop, a vote fanfare; very short hits, fired on cue |
| **Long form** (a Harold, a Montage): one hour, no stops, scenes that return | places and moods that run under whole scenes, slow cross-fades, a sound given back when a scene comes back an hour later |
| **Cabaret night**: a host, short games, a guest act | walk-on music per player, a game's own theme, applause, a big board with many groups |
| **Kids' matinee**: animals, magic, a chase | many hits fired fast and layered, loud ones kept level, a phone or tablet in a small hall |
| **Touring show**: a different venue each week | the same board on another laptop, from the board file and the folder, offline backstage |

The operator is a volunteer as often as a technician: some read every label,
some only tap. Decide who they are before the board is built.

## The calls

What reaches the operator during the show, with a breath to answer it:

- The MC: "Our theme: a submarine in trouble!" (a place, a mood).
- A player: "And suddenly, the phone rings." (a hit, now).
- The scene turns: from romance to horror in one line (cross from one mood to
  the next, without a hole and without a clash).
- The referee's whistle, a penalty, a vote (the same hit three times, fast).
- "Lights down": everything out, gracefully (Stop), or a blackout gag (Escape).
- A scene comes back from earlier (the same pad, set up as it was).
- Silence: a long scene with nothing to fire, the board left alone.

## What goes wrong

Let at least three of these happen during a run:

- The wrong pad is tapped, then the right one at once.
- A pad is tapped twice in a panic.
- A sound is still fading when the next call comes.
- The laptop's wifi drops, or the hall has none.
- The browser is reloaded mid-show, or the phone switches app and comes back.
- A sound in the library will not play.
- The volume is far too loud for the hall, mid-cue.
- The board is too small for tonight's show.
- An unlabelled key is pressed by mistake.
